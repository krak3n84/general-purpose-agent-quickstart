# SPCX fair-value scenario model

_Every figure here is an assumption, not a forecast. The point is to show what you'd have to believe to justify a price — not to predict one._

Anchors: 2025 revenue $18.7B, ~13.1B shares, project 5y to 2030.

## 1. Forward: what each story implies the stock is worth today

| Scenario | 2025→30 rev CAGR | 2030 revenue | exit P/S | discount | **implied value/share** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | 18% | $43B | 4x | 12% | **$7** |
| Base | 30% | $69B | 7x | 11% | **$22** |
| Bull | 45% | $120B | 11x | 10% | **$62** |

Range: **$7 (Bear) – $22 (Base) – $62 (Bull)**.

Scenario stories:
- **Bear** (→ $7): Starlink ARPU keeps falling, xAI stays a money pit, launch stays low-margin, growth decelerates toward 18%.
- **Base** (→ $22): Starlink keeps ~30% growth, xAI turns breakeven-ish, Starship adds real launch revenue. Roughly consensus.
- **Bull** (→ $62): xAI revenue ramps toward Goldman's number, Starlink monopoly compounds, multiple stays premium. Everything works.

## How to read this

- If a price requires a 2030 revenue *above* Goldman's already-aggressive, conflicted $474B estimate, you are paying for a near-perfect outcome — that is the overpaying zone.
- The **Base** forward value is the honest midpoint; the gap between it and today's price is your margin of safety (positive) or your overpayment (negative).
- Change the assumptions at the top of the script and re-run. If you can't defend the assumptions that justify a price, don't pay it.

_Not investment advice. Assumptions are illustrative; verify the live quote and the latest financials before any real-money decision._
